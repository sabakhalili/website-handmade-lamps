Our story

Where it started

Walk into a hundred-year-old home in India. Look up. The pillars are carved, the beams have patterns, wood shaped the way it used to be. Somewhere along the way, we stopped building like this. We stopped decorating like this too.

Dock9 Studio started with one question: why did we stop?

Why reclaimed wood

We don't cut new timber. We work with reclaimed wood, pieces that have already lived a life, and shape them into lamps by hand. Nothing new is felled, so the price stays fair, and the hard work behind every piece stays worth it.

"Every wooden piece has lived once. We give it another life." — Dock9 Studio
